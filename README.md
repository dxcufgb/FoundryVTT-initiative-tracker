The initiative order without half the screen. While a combat runs, a slim tracker over the map shows the combatants' tokens in turn order: as a horizontal or vertical row, a tiny **now / next** pill, or a **semicircle** with the current combatant in the centre and the turns before and after it fading out along the arc. Tokens slide and resize into place as turns change, a line marks where the next round starts, and a defeated combatant fades to grey under a skull. Turn-order numbers can sit on the tokens themselves, so nobody has to look away from the map.

Every player turns it on and sets it up their own way: layout, size, token shape and sizes, background, highlight colour, health bars and buttons, previewed live as they change the settings. Drag it anywhere by its grip; it snaps to the middle of the screen. Players get one red **End turn** button on their turn. The GM decides whose health players may see.

**Foundry VTT:** v13 · **System:** any

## Installation

In Foundry: **Add-on Modules → Install Module**, paste this link into **Manifest URL** at the bottom, and click **Install**:

```
https://github.com/dxcufgb/FoundryVTT-initiative-tracker/releases/latest/download/module.json
```

Enable it in your world. Each player turns the tracker on under **Configure Settings → Dxcufgb's Initiative Tracker → Show the initiative tracker** (or with a key they set under **Configure Controls**); it then shows while a combat runs.
